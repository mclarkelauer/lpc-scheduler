How to use this page
  • Now & next shows what is on in each room right now and what follows, while the conference is running. It updates every minute.
  • Full schedule lists one day at a time; narrow it by track or room. Typing in the search box searches all three days at once, including speakers and abstracts; clicking a day leaves the search. Sessions that are over are greyed and marked "ended".
  • Abstract under a session opens its description, and details ↗ opens the session on lpc.events.
  • Every session has two buttons. ☆ Interested puts it in My picks, for example to watch the recording later. ✓ Attending says you plan to be there, and counts as interested too. Click ✓ again to keep a session as interested only, or the star again to drop it.
  • My picks in its List view shows everything you marked, or only what you are attending, or only what you are interested in but not attending, which is your list of talks to catch up on afterwards. Attended sessions that clash are flagged. You can copy the picks as text, download the attended ones as a calendar file, or clear them.
  • Calendar, the other view in My picks, lays your picks out as a timetable of the three days: solid blocks for what you are attending, dashed for what you are only interested in. On a phone, swipe sideways for the other days. Click an empty time to see every session in that half hour and mark the ones you want. Click a pick to see its details and abstract. A red line marks the current time during the conference.
  • Picks are saved in this browser only. Copy share link or Show QR code in My picks copies them once to another device, or to someone else; the browser that opens the link can add them to its own picks or replace them.
  • Calendar sync puts the sessions you are attending in your calendar app and keeps all your picks the same in every browser you use:
    1. In My picks, click Turn on on the "Calendar subscription and sync" line. From then on the line shows whether sync is on and when it last synced; click its name to open or close the rest.
    2. With it open, subscribe your calendar app to the Calendar link. In Google Calendar, copy it and add it under Other calendars, From URL. In most other apps, Open in calendar app does it. It holds the sessions you are attending. This link is read-only, so it is also the one to give to someone who wants to follow them.
    3. For your phone or another browser, open the line, click Link another browser, and scan the QR code or open the link there. Keep that link to yourself: it lets a browser change your picks.
    4. Mark sessions as usual, in any linked browser. The others catch up within a few minutes, or at once when you open the page, and changes made without a connection are sent when it returns. Your calendar shows changes when the app next refreshes, which Google Calendar may only do once or twice a day.
    5. To stop, open the line. Unlink this browser stops syncing in one browser and lets you keep or clear the picks in it. Delete my synced picks removes them from the server, unlinks every browser and ends the calendar link.
  • All times are Prague time. The schedule is a snapshot of lpc.events: the footer says when it was taken, and reloading the page gets the newest one.
